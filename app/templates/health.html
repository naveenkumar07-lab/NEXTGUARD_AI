Health Score Formula Specification

The Composite Network Health Score evaluates operational stability across 3 weighted vectors:

Score = 100 - PenaltyLatency - PenaltyJitter - PenaltyPacketLoss
  • Latency Penalty: Scaled above baseline 20ms up to 250ms (max -40 points).
  • Jitter Penalty: Scaled above 5ms up to 50ms (max -30 points).
  • Loss Penalty: Linear deduction per dropped probe (max -30 points).
